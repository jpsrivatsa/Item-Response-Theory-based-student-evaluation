2 Parameter Logic Based Evaluation

Principle

Considers difficulty and discriminancy of the question as a parameter

The 2-parameter (2-PL) IRT model extends the 1-PL Rasch model by estimating an item discrimination parameter (a) and an item difficulty parameter. The discrimination parameter is similar to an item-total correlation and typically ranges from ~0.5 to 2. .
